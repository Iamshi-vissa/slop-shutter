# notes from the move

Took three weeks longer than planned. The landlord kept the deposit "pending inspection", which as far as I can tell means pending him remembering we exist. I've emailed twice.

Things that went wrong, roughly in order:

- the van was booked for the wrong Saturday (my fault)
- Sam's bookshelf didn't fit up the stairs so it's in the garage now, on its side, full of paint tins
- no internet for nine days. nine! I did a lot of reading. Finished the Le Guin I'd been stalling on since March and started the one about the lighthouse keepers, which is slower than I expected but good

The kitchen is tiny. The oven runs hot by about twenty degrees so everything I bake comes out a bit burnt on top and raw in the middle, which is a talent, honestly. Someone on the forum said to put a pizza stone on the bottom shelf to even it out. Haven't tried it yet.

Upside: the light in the back room in the afternoon. I didn't notice it when we viewed the place because it was raining. It's lovely. I've put the desk there and I'm writing this from it.

Still to do — buy curtains, chase the deposit, find out which of the four bins is for glass (the council website contradicts the sticker on the bin, and the neighbour contradicts both).
